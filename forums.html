About this prototype

Demonstration prototype

All cinema records are fictional, except the map and research table, which use real sites in Brisbane, Sydney, Melbourne and regional Queensland and NSW with cited sources. A design demonstration for review (CR-011). Front end only, except the Cinema Community forums, which store member accounts and posts once they are switched on. Illustrations were drawn for the prototype and are not historical photographs.
