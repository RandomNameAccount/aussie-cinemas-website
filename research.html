How confidence is recorded

Location confidence

  • Published site point: coordinates published for this cinema by a heritage register, the Dictionary of Sydney or Wikipedia, or a licensed government dataset (Property address Queensland; NSW State Heritage Register centroids). The source is named in each record. Not checked against the historic footprint or entrance.
  • Suburb only or City centre only: the marker sits at a published reference point, not at the building.
  • Not mapped: the sources don't give enough to place it.

Evidence confidence

  • Heritage register confirms name and address An official heritage register entry (Brisbane City Council, NSW State Heritage Inventory or Victorian Heritage Database) was read directly.
  • Two published sources agree Two independent published sources agree.
  • Single secondary source One secondary source, usually the volunteer-edited Cinema Treasures.
  • Sources disagree Shown alongside any level when sources disagree.

Limits of this research

  • The Queensland Heritage Register pages refused automated reading, so the earlier entries are cited as reported by Wikipedia or Brisbane City Council. Records added on 22 November 2026 cite the register’s open dataset (CC BY 4.0) instead, and their map points come from licensed government datasets.
  • Trove and the State Library of Queensland could not be reached from the research environment.
  • The Victorian Heritage Database pages read give no coordinates, so Melbourne points come from Wikipedia. Sydney points come from the NSW State Heritage Inventory where it has an entry.
  • Cinema Treasures and Wikipedia are volunteer-edited secondary sources. Records that rest on them alone are marked preliminary.
  • Present-day street addresses may not match a historic entrance or building footprint.
