What the sources say

  • July 1893 Belmore Markets opened in the building (NSW State Heritage Inventory: Capitol Theatre (SHR 00391))
  • April 1916 Opened as the Hippodrome (NSW State Heritage Inventory: Capitol Theatre (SHR 00391), Cinema Treasures: Capitol Theatre (Sydney))
  • 7 April 1928 Opened as the Capitol, an atmospheric picture palace (NSW State Heritage Inventory: Capitol Theatre (SHR 00391), Cinema Treasures: Capitol Theatre (Sydney), Wikipedia: Capitol Theatre, Sydney)
  • November 1932 Closed (NSW State Heritage Inventory: Capitol Theatre (SHR 00391))
  • April 1933 Reopened (NSW State Heritage Inventory: Capitol Theatre (SHR 00391))
  • 1972 Greater Union lease ended (Cinema Treasures: Capitol Theatre (Sydney), Wikipedia: Capitol Theatre, Sydney)
  • 24 January 1995 Reopened as a live theatre after restoration (Cinema Treasures: Capitol Theatre (Sydney))
