How sure we are

Evidence: An official heritage register entry was read directly and confirms the cinema's name and address. Individual dates and events are cited to their own sources below and are only as reliable as those sources.

Location: Location withheld from the public map while the coordinate's licence or provenance is reviewed.

Sources disagree: both versions are kept below. Neither has been chosen.

Address
1 Chapel Street (Heritage Database: 1 to 9 Chapel Street), St Kilda
Status today
Operating cinema. Operating single-screen cinema; Cinema Treasures says Palace Cinemas reopened it on 25 June 2015.
Location
Location withheld. Withheld from the public map (Phase A, 24 November 2026): the only point available was published in the cited Wikipedia article, which is not licensed for permanent reuse. To be replaced with a licensed point (for example Vicmap Address, CC BY 4.0) before release.
Evidence
Heritage register confirms name and address
Heritage listing
Victorian Heritage Database place 1818; National Trust classification 1 October 1987 (place 66465). Register number not shown on the pages read.
Sources disagree
  • Opening day: 3 April 1936 (Heritage Database, Wikipedia) or 13 April 1936 (Cinema Treasures).
  • Greek-language films from 1969 (Heritage Database) or 1967 (Cinema Treasures).
  • Reopening after the 1982 closure: September 1982 (Cinema Treasures) or 1983 (Wikipedia).
  • Earlier Diamond cinema on the site: 1908 (Cinema Treasures) or opened 29 July 1912 (Wikipedia).

Both versions are kept. Neither has been chosen.

What the sources say

  • 1908 (Cinema Treasures) or 29 July 1912 (Wikipedia) Earlier cinema on the site, the Diamond (Cinema Treasures: Astor Theatre, Wikipedia: Astor Theatre, Melbourne)
  • 3 April 1936 (Heritage Database, Wikipedia) or 13 April 1936 (Cinema Treasures) Opened (Victorian Heritage Database: The Astor Theatre, Wikipedia: Astor Theatre, Melbourne, Cinema Treasures: Astor Theatre)
  • 1969 (Heritage Database) or 1967 (Cinema Treasures) Began screening Greek-language films (Victorian Heritage Database: The Astor Theatre, Cinema Treasures: Astor Theatre)
  • February 1982 Closed (Cinema Treasures: Astor Theatre, Wikipedia: Astor Theatre, Melbourne)
  • September 1982 (Cinema Treasures) or 1983 (Wikipedia) Reopened (Cinema Treasures: Astor Theatre, Wikipedia: Astor Theatre, Melbourne)
  • 25 June 2015 Reopened under Palace Cinemas (Cinema Treasures: Astor Theatre)
