What the sources say

  • 15 March 1929 Opened as a picture palace (Wikipedia: Regent Theatre, Melbourne)
  • 29 April 1945 Damaged by fire (Wikipedia: Regent Theatre, Melbourne)
  • 19 December 1947 Reopened (Wikipedia: Regent Theatre, Melbourne)
  • 1 July 1970 Closed by Hoyts (Wikipedia: Regent Theatre, Melbourne, Victorian Heritage Database: Regent Theatre)
  • 17 August 1996 Reopened as a live theatre (Wikipedia: Regent Theatre, Melbourne)
