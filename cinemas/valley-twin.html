What the sources say

  • 1923 Building completed for shops and businesses (Brisbane City Council Heritage Register: Valley Twin Cinema)
  • around 1975 Converted into a twin cinema (Brisbane City Council Heritage Register: Valley Twin Cinema)
  • 2004 Operating as a cinema (Cinema Treasures: Globe Theatre)
  • by 2011 Renamed the Globe Theatre (Cinema Treasures: Globe Theatre)
