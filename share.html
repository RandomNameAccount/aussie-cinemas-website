    • You give the archive permission to preserve and show it.
    • If you're not sure who owns a photograph, tell us. It stays private until we check.

    About this prototype

    Demonstration prototype

    All cinema records are fictional, except the map and research table, which use real sites in Brisbane, Sydney and Melbourne with cited sources. A design demonstration for review (CR-011). Front end only: no accounts, no database, and nothing is sent or saved. Illustrations were drawn for the prototype and are not historical photographs.
