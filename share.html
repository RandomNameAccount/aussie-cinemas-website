Share your story

Your memories, photographs and papers help us tell the story of Australian cinemas. Uncertain details are welcome: "about 1962" or "I'm not sure" are good answers.

    Your privacy

    • We never show your email or phone number.
    • You choose how you are credited.
    • Living people mentioned in stories can be shown by initials only.
    • You can ask for your contribution to be removed at any time.

    Copyright

    • You keep the copyright in what you share.
    • You give the archive permission to preserve and show it.
    • If you're not sure who owns a photograph, tell us. It stays private until we check.

    Prefer to talk?

    In the real archive, volunteers will be able to record your story by phone or in person.
