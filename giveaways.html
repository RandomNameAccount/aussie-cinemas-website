Who can enter (proposed)

  • Australian residents aged 18 or over
  • Verified member account, one entry per person
  • Staff of the organising cinema and their immediate families can’t enter
  • Free to enter, with no purchase necessary

What the organising cinema must supply

  • Legal name and ABN
  • Prize details and value
  • Eligibility and exclusions
  • Entry period, draw and winner notification
  • Redraw rules for unclaimed prizes
  • Permits for each state or territory where required
  • Full terms based on the approved template

Audit record

Approval, entry numbers, the draw, winners, notifications and redraws are all logged and kept for the period the law requires.

About this prototype

Demonstration prototype

All cinema records are fictional, except the map and research table, which use real sites in Brisbane, Sydney and Melbourne with cited sources. A design demonstration for review (CR-011). Front end only: no accounts, no database, and nothing is sent or saved. Illustrations were drawn for the prototype and are not historical photographs.
