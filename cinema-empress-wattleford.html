Empress Theatre

Wattleford, New South Wales · Picture theatre · Repurposed

An art deco picture palace rebuilt on the site of an earlier hall, twinned in the 1970s and now an arts centre.

Opened
March 1928 Strongly supported
Closed
1991 Confirmed historical fact
Capacity
1,140 seats (1928)
Former names
  • Wattleford Picture Hall (about 1916 to 1927)
  • Empress Twin (1975 to 1991)
