Cinema profile

  • Business name and ABN, as verified
  • Public address, phone, website and accessibility information
  • Opening hours and screens
  • Verification checked by an administrator and renewed every 12 months

How cinemas are verified

  1. The owner applies with the legal business name, ABN, address, phone and website.
  2. We check the ABN is active and the email matches the cinema’s website.
  3. An administrator calls the cinema’s public number, or checks a document.
  4. Only verified cinemas can publish screenings, polls or promotions.
