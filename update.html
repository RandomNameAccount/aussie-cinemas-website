What happens next

  1. Your suggestion goes into a queue. It is not published.
  2. A moderator reads it and marks it accepted, declined or needing more information. You see the result here.
  3. An editor checks the evidence and updates the cinema’s record. A new map pin needs a street address that matches the state’s official address data.

Suggestions are private to you and the moderators. See the community guidelines and privacy notice.
