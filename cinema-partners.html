Verified cinema

1. Free basic listing

Every verified cinema gets a profile, followers, polls and screenings in What’s On and the calendar, at no cost. Free listings are ordered by date and distance only.

Sponsored

3. Regional sponsored placement

One “supported by” slot per region each month, shown in What’s On for that region.

Sponsored

4. Homepage banner

One banner below What’s On Near You, never above the map or inside archive pages. Static artwork with alt text, no animation.

Sponsored

5. Newsletter sponsorship

One sponsor block per monthly issue, sent only to members who chose to receive the newsletter.

Rules that can’t be bought

  • Every paid placement says Sponsored in words, not colour alone.
  • No advertising inside archive records, research pages or archive search results, and no effect on rankings or evidence ratings.
  • Advertisers can’t use archive evidence badges or curator language.
  • No tracking across sites and no third-party ad networks. Reports show views and clicks only, never who you are.

About this prototype

Demonstration prototype

All cinema records are fictional, except the map and research table, which use real sites in Brisbane, Sydney, Melbourne and regional Queensland and NSW with cited sources. A design demonstration for review (CR-011). Front end only: no accounts, no database, and nothing is sent or saved. Illustrations were drawn for the prototype and are not historical photographs.
