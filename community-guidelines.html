Cinema Community

Community guidelines and privacy notice

The rules for the Cinema Community forums, and what we do with the information you give us.

Guidelines

  1. Be kind and on topic. Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, abuse, threats or discrimination.
  2. Protect people’s privacy. Don’t post anyone’s address, phone number, email or private details, including your own. Ask before naming living people in personal stories.
  3. Say how you know. Memories are welcome. If you’re unsure of a date or name, say so; other members may help.
  4. Respect copyright. Link to photos and articles rather than copying them, unless you own them or have permission.
  5. No advertising or spam. Cinemas can promote screenings through their verified cinema account, not the forums.
  6. Nothing illegal or harmful. We remove it and may report it to the authorities or the eSafety Commissioner.
  7. One account per person, aged 16 or over.

How moderation works

  • A moderator checks each new member’s first two posts before they appear.
  • Anyone signed in can report a post. Moderators review reports and may hide posts, give a warning or suspend an account.
  • Every moderation action is recorded.
  • To ask about a moderation decision, contact the site team (contact details are being set up).

Privacy notice for members

  • What we collect: your email address (to send sign-in links), the display name you choose, your confirmation that you are 16 or over, your posts and reports, and the times you made them. Our database provider also keeps short-term technical logs such as IP addresses for security.
  • What is public: your display name and your posts. Your email address is never shown.
  • Why: to run your account, show your posts, keep the forums safe and respond to reports.
  • Where: stored with Supabase, our database provider, in its Australian (Sydney) region.
  • Email: we only email you sign-in links unless you choose to receive more.
  • Your choices: you can delete your own posts at any time. To delete your account or get a copy of your information, contact the site team.

These guidelines may change as the forums grow. Material changes will be announced on this page.
