Classic-film voting

Help a cinema choose its next classic screening. Not open yet.

Verified cinemaOpen · closes Sunday 13 December 2026, 8:00 pm AEDT
Cinema Community isn’t open yetScreenings, voting, member and cinema accounts, giveaways and advertising are not active, and no sample listings are shown. The forums will be the first part to open, with free member accounts.
No polls are openVerified cinemas will be able to run polls once cinema and member accounts open.

What this will do

  • A verified cinema lists a few films and a closing date; each member gets one vote per poll, and votes can’t be changed.
  • Results are published when the poll closes.
  • A vote is not a booking: whether a film screens depends on rights and print availability.
