The checked sources confirm the venue and its town or suburb. Its street address has not been verified, so it is not shown. Only verified properties get an exact pin. This listing is shown on the map at the centre of its town or suburb, not where the building stood.

About this listing

The Nepean began as an open-air picture show on a vacant block in Penrith on 9 March 1911, with seats for about 1,000 people and screenings in summer only. Its owners later converted neighbouring buildings into the indoor Nepean Theatre.
